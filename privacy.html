Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 July 2026

What Task Garden stores

Task Garden stores the information needed to provide the app: your account email, tasks, habits, goals, coin balance, the items you place in your garden, and any feedback report you choose to submit. A feedback report can include its type, rating, comment, and an optional contact email.

Why the information is used

Your information is used only to authenticate your account, keep your data separate from other users, synchronise your garden across devices, operate the features you choose to use, and review feedback reports that you choose to send.

Where data is processed

Task Garden is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Supabase for authentication and database storage. These services may process technical connection information necessary to deliver the site and their services.

Browser storage and cookies

The current app does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It uses essential browser local storage to remember your signed-in session. This is not used for advertising or profiling.

Sharing

Task Garden does not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the hosting and database providers needed to run the app.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays in the service while your account exists. Reset progress clears your tasks, habits, goals, placed garden items, and coins while keeping the account itself. Delete account is intended to permanently remove the account and associated app data once the account-deletion function is configured.

Your choices

You can delete individual tasks, habits, goals, and garden items in the app. You can reset your progress from Settings. For questions about this project, use the contact details available through the Credentials page.

Task Garden is an independent personal project. This text is a practical project policy, not a substitute for legal advice tailored to a public product.
